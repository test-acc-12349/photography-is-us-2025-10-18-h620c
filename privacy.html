Cookies and Tracking Technologies

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit our Site. They help us recognize you, remember your preferences, and track your interactions with our Site. Cookies can be either session-based (temporary) or persistent (remain until deleted).

Types of Cookies We Use

  • Essential Cookies: Required for basic Site functionality, security, and authentication. These cookies cannot be disabled.
  • Performance Cookies: Used to analyze how you use our Site and to measure Site performance and effectiveness.
  • Functional Cookies: Remember your preferences and choices to provide a personalized experience on our Site.
  • Marketing Cookies: Used to display personalized advertisements and track marketing campaign effectiveness.

Managing Cookies

Most web browsers allow you to control cookies through their settings. You can choose to:

  • Accept all cookies
  • Reject all cookies
  • Receive a notification when a cookie is set
  • Delete cookies from your device

Other Tracking Technologies

In addition to cookies, we may use other tracking technologies such as web beacons, pixels, and similar technologies to collect information about your browsing activities and interactions with our Site. These technologies work in conjunction with cookies to provide us with valuable insights into how users engage with our content and services.
