About Photography Is Us

Photography Is Us was founded in 2010 by a group of passionate photographers who recognized a critical gap in the market. We noticed that professional-quality equipment was either prohibitively expensive or difficult to source reliably. Our founders, each with over 15 years of photography experience, decided to create a company that would bring professional-grade photography equipment to photographers of all levels. What started as a small operation has grown into a trusted global brand, serving over 50,000 photographers across 195 countries. Our journey has been defined by our unwavering commitment to quality, innovation, and customer satisfaction.

Our mission is simple yet profound: to democratize professional photography by providing exceptional equipment at fair prices, backed by outstanding customer service. We believe that every photographer—whether a beginner picking up a camera for the first time or a seasoned professional—deserves access to reliable, high-quality tools. Our core values of integrity, excellence, and innovation guide every decision we make. We rigorously test every product, maintain long-term relationships with our manufacturers, and continuously invest in research and development to bring you the latest photography technology. At Photography Is Us, we don't just sell equipment; we empower creative vision.
